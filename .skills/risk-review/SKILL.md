# Risk Review

Use this skill when a change needs a narrow risk pass before implementation, export, or release.

## Responsibilities

- Identify ambiguity in goals, rules, boundaries, and scenarios.
- Call out missing constraints, missing failure handling, and fragile assumptions.
- Surface test, rollout, or review gaps that still block confidence.

## Fixed Output

- Risk findings
- Missing constraints checklist
- Open questions for confirmation
